15-Minute Market Check

Screenshot a Kalshi 15-minute crypto market (or any up/down price market). Claude reads the target, time left and prices, the page checks the live price and works out the real odds after fees, and you get one answer: bet Up, bet Down, or skip.

Forecast window

Short forecasts come in 15-minute steps for the next hour. They work best with a 1-, 5- or 15-minute chart screenshot taken while the market is open.

  1. 1Read the screenshot
  2. 2Run indicators on the chart's prices
  3. 3Fetch real prices and news
  4. 4Weigh the evidence
Not financial advice. 15-minute crypto moves are close to random and Kalshi's prices are usually fair, so most checks should end in "skip": that's the page working, not failing. Each check uses your own Claude usage (two requests); live prices come from Crypto.com and CoinGecko, with Alpha Vantage as a fallback. Only bet what you can afford to lose.
